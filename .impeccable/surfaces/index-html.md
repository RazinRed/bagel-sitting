## Scope
Bagel's Sitter Guide, the whole single page (`index.html`). Mode: Operate (the sitter completes one visit), with reference sections read in place.

## Audience and task
First-time and regular sitters, phone in one hand, any light. Job: one complete, correct visit without messaging the owners. Worries to design out: the door, food-prep details, finding things, losing their place. Owner-pinned: cutesy and pink.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The visit is a kawaii memo pad with Bagel's sticker sheet: one current step at a time, and each finished step earns a sticker, so progress is a sheet emptying. It refuses the long scroll-and-tick checklist page with reference sections stacked underneath.

OWN-WORLD: Strawberry-milk ground, white memo sheets, hot-pink ink for numbers and quantities, deep plum text, butter-yellow and mint as sticker colours only. Die-cut stickers with a white keyline and soft drop shadow; dashed outline where a sticker will go. One rounded face (M PLUS Rounded 1c) for everything, CJK included. 8px grid, hairline pink rules, no boxes on boxes.

STORY: The sitter sees where they are (sticker sheet, step number), what to do now (photo, verb, poster-size quantity) and what comes next. They tap Done, a sticker lands, the next memo comes up. The door warning greets them at arrival and again before leaving. Reference details sit inside the step that needs them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with Bagel's face sticker, title and "3 stickers used · 11 to go"; the 14-slot sticker sheet beneath. Door note pinned above the memo on step 1 and step 14. Current memo: part label, step number, photo, title, quantity at 52px pink, one line of detail, and a full-width "Stick it! Done" button in the thumb zone. Next step peeks below.

FORM: Kawaii sticker sheet and memo pad, position 3 on the ordered list. seed:01913528eaebbb9e

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction
Done: the step's dashed slot receives a sticker (scale 1.15 to 1 with a small rotate, about 260ms ease-out) and the matching slot on the sheet fills; the memo then hands over to the next step. Reduced motion: instant swap, fade only.
