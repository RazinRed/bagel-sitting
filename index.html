Watch the front door

Bagel likes to nap on the mat right inside the front door. She doesn't usually try to run out, but we don't know how she'll act with someone new.

  • Coming in: open the door slowly and block the gap with your legs.
  • Saying hello: don't walk straight up to her. Come in low and sit down on the floor. She's much less scared of people at her level, so let her come to you.
  • Leaving: check where she is before you open the door.

Meal prep

wet food, krill oil and water
  1. Check the fridge first. Look for an opened can with the pink silicone lid on it. If there is one, use that. If not, open a new can: go for Kit Cat Tuna & Chicken Hearts, it's her favourite.
  2. Heat the water. On the glass kettle, press the lower right button (labelled 功能 / 取消) twice to start heating.
  3. Scoop the food. Use the white plastic spoon (kept in her bowl by the sink) to scoop ⅓ of the can into the bowl.
  4. Add krill oil. Press the pump bottle down halfway for ½ pump onto the food.
  5. Mix in the water. Pour some in and stir until the food is a thick, liquidy consistency, like a thick soup.
    Fresh can
    Use warm water, about 40°C. It should feel warm, not hot.
    Can from the fridge
    Use hot water to take the chill off the food.
  6. Cover the can. Put the pink silicone lid over the opened can and put it back in the fridge.
Grab the right bottle. The krill oil sits on the counter next to some of our own supplements. Bagel's is the silver bottle with the red "Hip & Joint" label and a pump top.

Serving

her dining corner

Put the bowl on the floor to the left of the white automatic feeder. That's where Bagel expects her wet food.

She might not eat it straight away, and that's okay. Leave the bowl out. It gets washed at the next visit.

Automatic feeder
Petkit Fresh Element Solo. It gives her dry kibble by itself.
Water fountain
The white water bowl to the right of the feeder. Check every visit that it has water and still has battery. If the battery is low, let us know in the group chat.
Churu treats
Inaba Ciao Churu tubes, in the clear tub with the pink lid on the kitchen counter.
Not eating? Squeeze a little Churu onto her wet food to tempt her. After playtime, you can also place a treat in front of her bowl.

Litter box

every visit
  1. Find it in the toilet at the back of the home.
  2. Scoop out any clumps and poop.
  3. Flush it down the toilet. We use flushable litter, so it's safe.
  4. Close the toilet lid afterwards.

Clean up

quick and easy

Wash her bowl and put it back on the counter next to the kitchen sink, with the white spoon resting inside.

If she hasn't finished, leave the bowl out and wash it at the start of your next visit. On your last day, just leave it; we'll clean it when we're back.

Her bowl: the raised white ceramic one with a blue wave and fish pattern on the stand.

Playtime

optional, 10 to 15 minutes

We have two wand toys, tucked into the gap between the sofa cushion and the left armrest. Slide them back in when you're done.

Bagel can be shy with new people, so if she isn't in the mood, that's completely fine. No pressure!

Catch her eye

Wiggle the wand quickly to get her attention.

Hide and seek

Once she's watching, move the toy behind the left or right side of the sofa. She usually chases when it disappears.

Tail tickle

Lightly touch the toy to her lower back or tail. She'll chase it or flop down and bite it.

Treat time! After playtime, give Bagel a treat. They're in the clear container on the table next to the 3D printer. If she hasn't eaten her wet food yet, place the treat in front of her wet food bowl to lead her to it.
